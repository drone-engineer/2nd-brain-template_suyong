# 멀티로터 비행체의 실내 자율비행 실시간 비행실험 결과

## 초록

A multi-rotor is an autonomous flying robot with multiple rotors. Depending on the number of the rotors, multi-rotors are categorized. Multi-rotor is capable of vertical take-off and has rapid mobility, it is used more widely than other UAVs for reconnaissance and surveillance in hostile urban areas surrounded by high story buildings. In this paper we derive nonlinear control equation based on multi-rotor dynamics. Then, based on derived control equations, For verifying and validating the control models, nonlinear control technique experiments are carried out.
