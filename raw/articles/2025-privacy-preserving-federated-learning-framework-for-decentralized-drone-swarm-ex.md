# Privacy-Preserving Federated Learning Framework for Decentralized Drone Swarm Exploration

**DOI:** 10.1109/IIPEM65914.2025.11548338

## 초록 (Abstract)

Autonomous multi-drone exploration systems have been based on centralized control topologies which impose fundamental weaknesses: single point of vulnerability to catastrophic system breakdown, significant privacy breaches to data centralization, bandwidth scaling behavior of O(n2) and Byzantine failure modes in adversarial environments. This paper presents a comprehensive Byzantine-strong federated learning system designed as privacy-preserving decentralized exploration of drone swarm in complex 3D environments. The architecture removes server contact by gossip-based distributed consensus to ensure that autonomous unmanned aerial vehicles can learn the best exploration policies and provide differential privacy guarantees (ε-DP) and raw sensor locality. The results of the experiments indicate the high effectiveness of the proposed framework compared to their centralized counterparts: 95% spatial coverage (vs. 85%), 15% energy savings, 22-27% reduction in convergence time, and differential privacy guarantees (ε = 0.1). A unified system of web-based visualization allows real-time monitoring and repeatable research.
